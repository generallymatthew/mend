# 1 Million Cups Presentation

## The Complete Spoken Story (With the Cold-Open Hook)

### 1. The Cold Open & Intro (The Spark)

"One day, while sitting at home messing around in Claude Code, my kids came downstairs in the middle of an argument about who stole whose charger. And a little lightbulb went off in my head, and I thought: 'I wonder if AI could actually handle this really boring, messy, confusing kind of human problem.'

Hi, I'm Matthew. I'm a UX and product designer, and I've lived and worked here in Durham, North Carolina for the last 15 years. My three passions are technology, design, and human behavior.

Now, a lot of people think understanding human behavior is an innate skill, just common sense, but it definitely didn't come naturally for me, and I'll give you an example from early in my career on my very first UX job."

### 2. The Early-Career Hook (Why This Matters)

"At that startup, there was a Hunger Games scenario set up by my manager that pitted three of his direct reports against each other while he went on paternity leave. At that time, there were no good options. I asked my friends, and they gave me bad advice because they just validated my anger. I knew I couldn't go to my manager since he's the one who set it up, and I knew I couldn't go to HR because, even as young as I was, I knew that wasn't a safe place to go.

What I really needed back then was an unbiased confidant who understood workplace dynamics and had my best professional interests at heart, not just telling me what would make me feel good in the moment, and without creating a permanent paper trail."

### 3. The Product (What Mend Is)

"So I started building Mend. i understood that in order for anyone in a work setting to use this in an honest way they had to trust that what was ever said to this app would never be seen by anyone else at the company expecially hr and management. It's an off-the-record sounding board specifically built to help people work through workplace friction, looking at structural root causes like unclear expectations or colliding priorities instead of just validating anger like a generic chatbot does. It runs strictly in volatile memory. Like a dissolving stitch, it holds things together while you sort it out, and the second you're done, it wipes to zero. No saved transcripts, no HR dashboard. Nothing can ever be reviewed afterwards because nothing exists after the conversation ends"

### 4. The Struggle & The Breakthrough (The HR / IT / Legal Bind)

"Now, here's where I hit a wall as a builder. I knew employees and managers would use this, and data backs that up. But i also knew that it would be hard to sell to individuals. My ideal customer is a progressive hr team that values solutions over control. What I couldn't figure out was: why would HR ever pay for a tool they aren't allowed to control or see? Every other hr enterprise software is built around monitoring and control, and the data shows that because of that the usage rate hovers around 3% usage rate amoung employees

Then I started looking at how HR and other teams are reacting to AI right now, and everything clicked. One of their biggest headaches today with regards to AI is employees pasting sensitive Performance Improvement Plans, PIPs, and internal disputes straight into personal Gemini or ChatGPT accounts on their phones. These teams hate this because that sensitive company information is sitting on unsecured personal devices and consumer servers, creating massive data leak risks and leaving permanent chat histories that can be subpoenaed in a lawsuit.
There is no good way to stop it from happening either.

That was my unlock. Mend solves the problem for everyone at the table: employees and managers get access to good advice on how to handle social dynamics and hard conversations in a off-the-record, zero-trace sanctuary they actually trust, while HR, IT, and Legal get sensitive company context off personal devices and public AI servers. Reducing their potential liabilty, which has insurance rates implications"
