# Outreach emails

Draft copy for recruiting beta testers (not buyers) via personal network. See `docs/pitch-strategy/25_SEAM_SOLO_MANAGER_COHORT_AND_OUTREACH_STRATEGY.md` for the broader tester/buyer strategy this fits into.

## Mass email to personal network (beta testers, free)

**Subject: Quick favor, looking for people to try something I built**

Hey all,

I need your help. I've been building a tool called Seam, a way to talk through a difficult work conversation with an AI beforehand, for managers or anyone who deals with these situations. It's free and still in early development.

What I need help with:

1. Testing it: https://trymend.solutions. Try it out and tell me what's wrong, what's confusing, or what doesn't feel accurate.
2. Forward it to anyone you think might benefit from it.

Thanks in advance,
Matthew

## Welcome & rollout email (paid buyer, sent manually after Stripe checkout)

Sent by hand from the support inbox once Stripe's "successful payment"
notification lands — see
[docs/pitch-strategy/34_STRIPE_CHECKOUT_SETUP.md](../docs/pitch-strategy/34_STRIPE_CHECKOUT_SETUP.md)
for the checkout flow this follows from. This is the only message a buyer
gets; there's no portal or login to follow up with afterward, so it has
to carry everything they need in one email.

**Subject: Your Mend access code — you're set up**

Hi [first name],

Thanks for setting Mend up for [company name]. Payment's confirmed — here's
everything your team needs.

**Your access code: `[ROTATING-CODE]`**

Share this however you'd normally share something company-wide — pinned in
your all-hands Slack channel, in your onboarding doc, in a benefits email.
Anyone at [company name] can use it to open Mend with no separate login
and no account tied to their name.

**What to tell people, if you want a one-line version:**
"We've set up Mend, a private place to think through a work conflict
before it escalates. No login, no company record of who uses it or what
they say — not even we can see it."

**What you'll get going forward, also by email, nothing to log into:**
- A monthly digest with aggregate usage (sessions run, estimated hours
  reclaimed) — no names, no transcripts, no way to drill down to an
  individual.
- Your Stripe receipt for billing records.
- This inbox for anything else — a new code if someone leaves and you
  want to rotate it, a question, support.

**One thing worth knowing upfront:** we genuinely can't see what gets
typed into a session, and we built it that way on purpose, so don't
expect us to ever be able to tell you who said what. That's the whole
point.

Reply here anytime.

Matthew
