Know what you actually want to say, before you say it.

Something's bothering you about a coworker, or your manager, or how a project went down. You could vent to a friend who wasn't there, or say nothing and let it sit. Thimble is somewhere to think it through first — what's actually going on, and how to raise it without it blowing up.

What it's for

You don't need a verdict. You need to think it through once, out loud.

Before you say something
Talk through what happened before you bring it to the person, so it comes out the way you mean it.
What's the real issue
The thing that's annoying you is rarely the actual thing. Thimble helps you find what's underneath it.
A message you're unsure about
Before you hit send, check how it actually reads — not just how you meant it.

Why it's safe to use

This is yours. Nobody else sees it, including your manager.

Nothing goes to HR or your manager
What you talk through with Thimble stays with you. It's not a reporting channel, a performance log, or a complaint form — it's a place to think, with full autonomy over what you do with it afterward.
Nothing is recorded, except where the law requires it
If what's being discussed meets the legal standard for harassment, discrimination, or a physical threat, the session halts and routes to HR. Short of that line, there's no transcript sitting anywhere waiting to be pulled up later.
