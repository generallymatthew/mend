Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026 · Thimble LLC

The short version

  • We do not save your conversations. They are held in memory only while you are talking, then deleted.
  • Your employer does not see what you said. They get usage counts, never content.
  • To reply to you, your words pass through the AI and voice services listed below.
  • We do not sell data, and there are no advertising trackers.

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how Thimble LLC ("Thimble," "we," "us") handles information when you visit our website, use a Thimble app, buy a subscription, or email us. It applies to both Customers (companies that buy Thimble) and users (their employees and managers).

2. Your conversations

When you talk with Thimble by typing or by voice, here is what happens to what you say:

  • Held in memory only. Your conversation is kept in our server's working memory so Thimble can follow along. We do not write it to a database, a file, or a backup.
  • Deleted when you're done. Closing the tab tells our server to delete it straight away. If that signal never arrives (for example, your browser crashes), it is deleted automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity. A server restart also erases it.
  • Sent to an AI model to write each reply. We send the conversation so far to Anthropic, which runs the Claude model that writes Thimble's replies. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Voice. If you use voice, your audio is streamed to Deepgram to turn it into text, and Thimble's replies are turned into speech by Deepgram or OpenAI. We do not record or store the audio.
  • Video. If a video version of Thimble is turned on, the conversation also passes through Tavus, which generates the video.

These providers may keep data for a short time under their own policies, for example to detect abuse.

3. What your employer sees

Your employer (our Customer) never sees your conversations. Once a month, their designated contact gets a digest with usage totals for their company, such as how many sessions took place. It contains no names, no conversation content, and nothing that identifies a single person's session.

4. Safety notices

Thimble watches for a small set of serious situations, and it will always tell you before anything is sent:

  • Threats of violence or an immediate safety risk: Thimble tells you it is alerting your company's HR contact, and this happens whether or not you agree.
  • Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or illegal activity: Thimble asks whether you want your company's HR contact told. Nothing is sent unless you say yes.

An alert contains the category and general severity only. It does not include your name, a session identifier, or quotes from your conversation. We keep a record that an alert was sent, along with its summary, so we can confirm it was delivered and prevent duplicates.

5. Information we do keep

Usage events. To understand whether Thimble is working, we record events such as a session starting or ending, the number of turns, whether you used text or voice, and whether you tapped a button saying a reply wasn't helpful. Each event carries a random session ID, a random device ID stored in your browser, and the company your access code belongs to. These events never contain what you said. We store them in a Google Sheet that only we can access.

Safety-check records. For quality and safety reviews, we log technical flags about each turn (for example, whether a safety check ran and what category it chose), tied to a random session ID. These records never include what you said.

Surveys. If you choose to answer a feedback survey, we store your answers in the same Google Sheet. Surveys are optional.

Usage costs. We log how much each AI and voice request costs us (for example, word counts and seconds of audio). These logs contain no conversation content.

6. Customers who buy Thimble

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your company name, billing contact, and payment details. We receive the name, email address, company, and payment status, but we never see or store full card numbers. We use the billing contact's email to send access codes, receipts, support replies, and the monthly digest. Our email is hosted by Zoho.

7. Cookies and browser storage

  • Access cookie. When you enter an access code, we set a cookie that remembers it for 30 days so you don't have to type it again.
  • Browser storage. The app stores a random session ID for the current tab and a random device ID, which group your usage events together. Neither contains anything you said.
  • Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell information to advertisers.

8. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run Thimble: Anthropic (AI replies), Deepgram and OpenAI (voice), Tavus (video, when enabled), Render (hosting, in the United States), Google (usage and survey storage, fonts), Stripe (payments), and Zoho (email). We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it, but because we do not store conversations, there are none for us to hand over.

9. How long we keep things

  • Conversations: until you close the tab, or 30 minutes of inactivity at most.
  • Usage events, safety-check records, and surveys: for as long as they help us improve Thimble. Ask us and we will delete them.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.

10. Security

All traffic to Thimble is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to our systems is limited to the people who run Thimble. No system is perfectly secure, which is why we keep as little as possible.

11. Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@withthimble.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we will honor them.

12. Children

Thimble is a workplace tool for adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top and email Customers' billing contacts before the change takes effect.
