# Mend: Executive Summary & Narrative

## Core Tagline

> **Heal the rift. Leave no trace.**

## Product Overview

Most workplace friction never reaches HR — it just festers, eroding productivity, culture, and retention. Mend provides a strictly confidential, AI-facilitated path for coworkers to resolve interpersonal friction privately and autonomously.

Like a dissolving suture, Mend holds a relationship together while it heals, then disappears completely. No persistent transcripts, no manager surveillance, no career penalties.

## The Strategic Problem: The "Missing Middle"

Every week, the average U.S. employee loses **2.8 hours** to workplace conflict (CPP Inc.). Today, employees face an unworkable binary choice:

1. **Vent to peers.** Releases emotion but fixes nothing systemic, and drags surrounding teammates into toxic backchannels.
2. **Escalate to HR or management.** High-friction and adversarial. It puts a permanent target on both parties' backs, risking either being labeled "difficult" or a "culture risk."

**Result:** most interpersonal friction sits unresolved in the "missing middle" — quietly bleeding organizational focus, damaging morale, and eroding cross-functional delivery velocity.

## The Core Thesis & Value Proposition

- **Outcomes over surveillance.** True resolution requires radical psychological safety. People only acknowledge blind spots and own mistakes when they know their words can never be weaponized against them.
- **Autonomous resolution.** Symmetrical turn-taking, solo caucusing, and decoupled agreements let colleagues reach durable alignment without authority intervention.
- **The dissolving suture.** Ephemeral, in-memory execution guarantees that once healing is achieved, no permanent dossier or career scar tissue remains.
