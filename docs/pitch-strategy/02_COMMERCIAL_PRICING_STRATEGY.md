# Mend: Commercial Pricing Strategy & Business Model

## The B2B Enterprise Pricing Paradox

Standard per-seat B2B SaaS pricing fails for conflict resolution. When access is restricted to managers or requires individual seat requests, employees avoid using it because of visibility risk and stigma. Access has to be all-hands to avoid signaling who is "in trouble."

### Recommended Enterprise Architecture: All-Hands Flat Tier (100 FTE benchmark)

- **Pricing:** $7.50 / employee / month (billed annually at $750/mo).
- **Annual Contract Value (ACV):** $9,000 / year.
- **Access model:** company-wide SSO, zero seat-gating — every employee has confidential, friction-free access.

## ROI Model & Financial Justification

| Metric Dimension | Financial Calculation | Annual Value Recaptured |
|---|---|---|
| Productivity recaptured | 2.8 hrs/wk lost (CPP Inc.) across 10% of affected staff at $47/hr blended wage | **$68,432/yr** |
| Manager capacity restored | 3.0 hrs/wk of frontline manager time freed from informal dispute counseling | **$18,720/yr** |
| Turnover prevention | Preempting a single mid-level resignation (Gallup benchmark: 1.5-2.0x salary) | **$45,000+** |
| **Gross total value** | Total estimated drag prevented | **$132,152+/yr** |
| **Net enterprise payback** | $132,152 value − $9,000 Mend license | **+$123,152 (14.6x ROI)** |

## Alternative Market Opportunities & Pricing Models

### 1. High-conflict co-parenting (court-mandated / family law)

- **Model:** $12-$18/month per family unit, split 50/50 between parents.
- **Evidentiary split:** private drafting and venting stay zero-trace in RAM; final mutually-signed schedules export as court-admissible PDFs.

### 2. Episodic groups (roommates, bands, sports teams)

- **The "truce ticket":** $10-$15 flat per resolved incident.
- **Group season pass:** $29/lease year (roommates) or $49-$79/project year (bands, creative duos).

## Incentives: Why Not a Gift Card?

Considered and rejected: a $5 Starbucks card (or similar) for using Mend.

- **Perverse incentives / farming.** A cash-equivalent reward encourages artificial or rushed sessions just to claim it, cheapening an act of professional maturity into a transactional task.
- **Breaks the zero-trace model.** Mend is built on hardware-isolated confidential enclaves that execute in volatile memory with no durable storage of identity or transcripts. Fulfilling a gift card requires an external API tied to an employee's email or account — exactly the identifying trail the product promises not to keep.
- **Undermines the maturity framing.** Paying someone $5 to talk to a peer implies resolving friction is a chore, not standard professional maintenance (see [07_CULTURAL_POSITIONING_AND_ADOPTION.md](07_CULTURAL_POSITIONING_AND_ADOPTION.md)).

The real incentive is intrinsic: reputation protection, immediate psychological relief, and reclaimed time — not a payout. (Note: stipends are normal for an external user-testing/research panel, as in [05_USER_TESTING_AND_COHORT_STRATEGY.md](05_USER_TESTING_AND_COHORT_STRATEGY.md); this is specifically about in-production enterprise usage.)
