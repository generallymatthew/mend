# Mend: User Testing & Validation Framework

## Solving the Asymmetry Problem

In conflict-mediation testing, recruiting Person A is straightforward; convincing Person B to join an experimental session is where conversion fails.

### The 3-Cohort Testing Breakdown (100 pairs / 200 testers)

1. **Cohort A — synthetic / roleplay pairs (30-40 pairs).** Testers select from a curated menu of 5 archetypal past workplace grievances they personally experienced (credit theft, Slack ghosting, cross-functional ambush, style clashes, compliance pushback). Personal memory supplies authentic emotional language and organic resistance.
2. **Cohort B — co-founder & early venture duos (30-40 pairs).** Early-stage partners navigating equity, burn, and operational friction. High real-world stakes, zero corporate HR bureaucracy.
3. **Cohort C — organic workplace coworkers (20-30 pairs).** Colleagues with active operational friction, using the full zero-trace workflow.

## 1-Human + 1-AI Adversary Alternative

To test emotional validity without scheduling bottlenecks: a human participant interacts with an AI Adversary while the AI Facilitator moderates. This eliminates partner-coordination friction while still giving a 100% genuine emotional response from the human tester.

## Evaluation Metrics (ADR Benchmarks)

- **Symmetry score** — post-session single-question verification of mediator neutrality.
- **Decoupled agreement rate** — percentage of participants who accept terms asynchronously when freed from live social pressure.
- **Watcher interception rate** — percentage of drafts intercepted and regenerated before reaching the screen.
- **14-day durability check** — verifying that agreements held up in actual sprint workflows without lingering friction.
