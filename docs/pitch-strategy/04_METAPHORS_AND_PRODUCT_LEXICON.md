# Mend: Textile & Sewing Metaphor Matrix

Sewing, tailoring, and textile metaphors give Mend a cohesive, craft-grounded vocabulary across the product.

## Structural Architecture & Core Engines

- **The Warp & Weft** (procedural turn-taking) — the horizontal/vertical threads that give cloth its structural integrity; describes the Facilitator's rigid, symmetrical turn-taking rules that keep a conversation from collapsing.
- **The Loom** (the ephemeral runtime enclave) — the confidential-computing hardware enclave where conversations are worked through in volatile memory and then cleared.
- **The Snips / Shears** (the non-configurable safety floor) — the deterministic circuit breaker that cuts a session off and escalates to HR when harassment or compliance lines are breached.
- **The Thimble** (the privacy firewall) — the protective barrier shielding the worker from getting pricked while handling high-friction material; represents the guarantee that management never sees sessions or records.

## Intake & Preparation Modules

- **The Spool** (the solo intake side conversation) — where a tangled strand is collected, smoothed, and wound into an orderly, tension-free thread before entering the machine.
- **Untangling / Teasing the Knot** (emotion-to-fact de-biasing) — teasing apart tight, snarled threads without snapping them; the intake prompt stripping emotional venting and accusations down to the functional operational blocker.
- **The Basting Stitch** (the practice rehearsal / mock session) — a temporary, loose stitch used to hold pieces in place for a trial fitting before final sewing; the rehearsal agent where an employee runs a low-stakes mock conversation against an AI counterpart first.
- **The Pattern** (the "tough talk cheat sheet") — the precise blueprint cut ahead of time to guide the conversation, keeping boundaries and talking points aligned.

## Mediation & Live-Room Tools

- **Tension Gauge** (the Watcher audit) — like a sewing machine's thread tension: too tight and it snaps, too loose and it bunches. The Watcher is the real-time gauge intercepting asymmetric blame or hostility before it snags the dialogue.
- **The Hem** (boundaries & scope setting) — folding and stitching a raw edge so it can't fray; establishing clear working boundaries, SLAs, and communication protocols.
- **Darning** (micro-friction repair) — reinforcing worn, thinning spots in fabric before a full tear develops; routine sprint check-ins or minor calibration before it festers into an HR dispute.
- **Gathering / The Gathering Stitch** (perspective mirroring) — pulling parallel threads together into one coherent shape; the Facilitator summarizing both sides into shared common ground.

## Resolution, Agreement & Outcomes

- **The Dissolving Suture** (core value proposition) — holds the dynamic securely while healing happens, then dissolves without leaving scar tissue, dossiers, or permanent career records.
- **Decoupled Knotting** (asynchronous agreement) — securing the final seam independently at each end, away from the tension of the live room, so neither party feels forced into a premature live concession.
- **The Seam** (the post-conflict working agreement) — the clean, reinforced line where two distinct panels now join and bear weight together.
- **Seamless** (the end state) — how the team operates once friction is resolved, re-knitted back into the social fabric without visible division or institutional baggage.

## Example: Feature Hierarchy in Marketing Copy

> **Mend: Precision Care for Frayed Teams**
> - The Spool — unwind raw tension in a private, 1:1 intake side conversation.
> - The Basting Stitch — test your framing in a low-stakes practice run before entering the room.
> - The Tension Gauge — real-time Watcher guardrails that keep dialogue grounded, fair, and safe.
> - The Dissolving Suture — ephemeral, zero-trace resolution that lets your team re-knit without management oversight or scar tissue.

## Naming decision: app renamed Darn -> Thimble (2026-10-19)

Considered renaming the Thimble persona to Darn instead. Rejected: Darn
works as a character/companion name ("I talked it through with Thimble")
and keeps the protective-guard meaning (a thimble keeps the needle from
pricking your finger, i.e. psychological safety), while Darn describes the
repair work itself, not an identity.

Settled the brand-architecture question instead by collapsing app and
persona into one name, so there's no 3-tier hierarchy (Mend -> app ->
persona) to confuse cold testers reading the About page. The app previously
codenamed **Darn** (solo conflict-resolution: intake -> joint session) is
now **Thimble** — same name as the facilitator persona, zero translation
layer: you open Thimble and you talk to Thimble. Darn itself stays reserved
for an action/mode name (e.g. "run a quick darn on this draft"), not a
product or persona identity. See `IDEAS.md`'s "App codenames" section for
the updated app-name list.

## Garment metaphor: mending the clothes, not the body

Core framing: Mend repairs the work persona (the "clothing" you put on for
work), not the person underneath. This maps to several existing frameworks
worth keeping in the back pocket for copy and positioning work:

- **Goffman's dramaturgy (front stage / back stage)** — the professional self
  is a performance for an audience; Mend is the back stage where you drop
  the act without being watched.
- **Jung's persona** — literally "actor's mask"; a functional social
  interface, not a lie. Friction snags the mask, not the core self.
- **Hochschild's emotional labor / surface acting** — corporate "fake it"
  performance is gear worn for a shift, not who you are; this protects the
  boundary between self-worth and the job.
- **Workwear / PPE** — the professional persona is social protective
  equipment; a bad interaction scuffs the gear, not the soul.
- **Tailored fit** — generic one-size-fits-all workplace norms chafe;
  mending/tailoring means adjusting the fit, not the body.
- **Visible mending (sashiko/boro)** — repaired fabric doesn't hide the
  tear, it reinforces the stress point with a visible stitch; a repaired
  working relationship doesn't pretend nothing happened, it makes the new
  boundary explicit and durable.

Copy frame this unlocks: deficit/medical language ("fix your personal
flaws") becomes craft/tailoring language ("reinforce the suit you wear to
work"). Treats the user as intact, not broken.

### De-stigmatizing vocabulary swaps

Use garment/craft terms instead of clinical or HR language:

| Stigmatized | Garment framing |
|---|---|
| Conflict resolution / dispute mediation | Calibrating working protocols / seam alignment |
| Airing grievances / calling someone out | Tracing where expectations and handoffs diverged |
| Forced apologies or handshakes | Co-authoring mutual SLAs and review rules |
| A damaged relationship | A temporary tear in the team dynamic |

Related principles already consistent with Mend's existing positioning:
repair doesn't require forced apologies or forced closeness — a cool,
functional working agreement (e.g. async-only, 24h response SLA) counts as
fully repaired. See the Dissolving Suture and Decoupled Knotting entries
above for the "no permanent record, no forced reconciliation" posture this
reinforces.
