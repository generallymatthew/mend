## Non-grant cash option: paid design-partner deposit
Find one small dev shop or design agency (20-40 people) in the Triangle. Offer a 30-day closed beta: flat $1,500-2,000 testing fee for unlimited squad access, custom prompt tuning for their team's dynamics, and a private teardown at the end. For an agency that size, that fee is an easy corporate-card expense and covers a full month of token/infra runway without a grant application at all.

## Framing by audience
- **Community/micro-grants** (Awesome Foundation, local SBC): empathetic, human. Hook: workplace conflict poisons everyday life; Mend gives people a private, judgment-free space to work through tension.
- **Innovation grants** (NC IDEA MICRO, Emergent Ventures): scientific, hypothesis-driven. Hook: testing whether an autonomous, zero-trace protocol resolves the "missing middle" of peer deadlocks faster than HR channels; need non-dilutive runway to fund inference cycles and validate agreement durability.
- **Accelerators** (CED, Launch Chapel Hill): operational, metric-driven. Hook: squads lose hours per week to unmanaged peer friction; Mend deflects triage from managers, preserves sprint velocity, avoids e-discovery liability.

## Blocker before applying to credit programs
Needs a working `founder@` (or similar) address on a custom domain — Anthropic and Google both require it, Gmail doesn't qualify. See the open domain question below.
