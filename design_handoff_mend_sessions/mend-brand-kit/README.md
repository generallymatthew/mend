# Mend brand kit (Pink + Evergreen)

## Logo
The mark is two overlapping circles that form a lowercase "m", with a knot at the base where the two sides meet.

Files in `logo/`:
- mend-mark-pink.svg: primary, on Oat or Evergreen backgrounds
- mend-mark-evergreen.svg: on Pink or light backgrounds
- mend-mark-oat.svg: on Evergreen or photography
- mend-app-icon-pink.svg / mend-app-icon-evergreen.svg: 1024×1024 app icons

Wordmark: "mend", all lowercase, Manrope ExtraBold (800), letter-spacing -0.05em. Place it to the right of the mark, vertically centered, with a gap of about 1/6 of the mark's width.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mend Pink | #F3829A | 243, 130, 154 | Primary: logo, fills, icons |
| Evergreen | #17372E | 23, 55, 46 | Ink: text, dark backgrounds, primary buttons |
| Honey | #E9B949 | 233, 185, 73 | Accent: highlights, "resolved" states |
| Oat | #F6F1E7 | 246, 241, 231 | Surface: page background |

Approved pairings:
- Pink mark + Evergreen text on Oat
- Pink mark + Oat text on Evergreen
- Evergreen mark + Evergreen text on Pink

Don't use Pink for body text on light backgrounds, because the contrast is too low. Use Evergreen instead.

## Typography
Manrope (Google Fonts): 800 for the wordmark and headlines, 500–700 for UI and body text.

## Brand themes
Resolution, impartiality, privacy and anonymity, people and collaboration.
