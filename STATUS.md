- **`intake-app`: three real fixes from one live debugging stretch -- a rate-limit bug that silently dropped Thimble's reply, stale browser caching that caused multiple false "it's not working" moments, and reload-starts-a-new-session per explicit request**:
  1. **Rate limiter was counting free local Whisper calls against the same budget as paid Claude calls**, reported live as "over a minute thinking and still has not responded." Server log showed the real cause: `POST /api/message` came back `429 Too Many Requests` -- Thimble's reply was never even attempted. `/api/voice/partial` (local Whisper only, never a paid API, deliberately chatty by design -- fires every ~1-2s of new audio during live listening) was counted by the same `RATE_LIMIT_MAX=20`/60s middleware that exists, per its own comment, specifically to protect paid-API endpoints. Verified directly: flooded it 25 times in a row (no 429s), then confirmed `/api/message` still succeeded immediately after, unaffected.

Reverted that. Real fix: once a turn's total audio passes `PARTIAL_MAX_TOTAL_SEC` (10s), simply stop resending live partials -- the caption freezes, but the already-proven-correct tail-patch at finalize still transcribes whatever's left in one clean pass once the turn actually ends. - **`intake-app`: fixed VAD never detecting silence under constant ambient noise (reported live: "it keeps listening after I've stopped talking, I don't know if it can hear my fan")** -- the real cause, and also the likely true explanation for the earlier 290s runaway clip (not confirmed as the cause then, but the same failure shape): calibration's fallback for "no genuinely-quiet sample ever appeared in the 400ms window" was to leave `vadThreshold` at the fixed 0.06 floor, on the assumption it might be the user talking rather than loud ambient noise. With a laptop fan sitting steadily above 0.06, that guess was wrong every time -- the threshold stayed below the fan's own level, so the fan read as nonstop speech and the turn could never detect real silence to end on. Now tracks the loudest level seen during calibration too, and when nothing quiet ever shows up, raises the threshold above that observed ambient level instead of leaving it stuck below it. Verified directly: simulated constant 0.08 "fan" noise through calibration (threshold correctly raised to 0.114), confirmed real "speech" at 0.25 still read as speaking, and confirmed the turn correctly ended once simulated speech stopped and only the fan noise remained -- the exact failure mode, fixed. Also added a hard `VAD_MAX_TURN_MS` (60s) backstop independent of VAD entirely, so no future cause of this same failure shape can ever again produce a multi-minute runaway recording that starves the whole server's CPU (verified directly: fed continuous loud "speech" well past 60s, turn force-ended on the cap). Known trade-off, explicit: someone who starts talking immediately in a genuinely quiet room could now get a threshold set near their own speaking volume, risking an early mid-word cutoff -- judged a cheaper failure than a recording that never stops.
