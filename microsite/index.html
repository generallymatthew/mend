The Cost of the Usual Options

Most conflict at work isn't personal, it's structural: unclear roles, competing incentives, bad process. Treating it as a character problem instead of a structural one is why it almost never gets fixed.

Say Nothing (Avoidance)The default choice. Tension festers, code reviews get delayed, and team collaboration quietly grinds to a halt.
Vent to a CoworkerFeels cathartic for an hour, but fixes nothing structurally and drags uninvolved teammates into backchannel politics.
Escalate to HR or ManagementCreates an adversarial paper trail for an issue that was simply two people out of sync, turning normal friction into a formal performance risk.
Resolve with MendTalk it through privately with Mend first, then work out how to actually fix it. Mend is trained on current conflict-resolution methodology and specifically on the distinction between a misunderstanding and harassment, so it knows when to help you work things out and when to route straight to the Safety Floor instead.

Resolving conflict directly is a sign of maturity, not a failure. The people who engage with it should be seen as the adults in the room, not as the source of the problem.

Radical Zero-Trace Privacy

Trust isn't a marketing tag. It is the technical foundation of the product, and the full breakdown lives on the Architecture page.

What is RecordedNothing. Sessions run in volatile memory and are never written to disk. We don't "record then delete": it is never saved in the first place.
What Managers SeeZero visibility into voluntary sessions. For sessions formally assigned by a manager, they receive only a binary confirmation: "Resolved" or "Unresolved", never conversation content.
The Non-Configurable Safety FloorTotal privacy for everyday peer friction, backed by an immutable legal boundary. Any detection of Title VII harassment, discrimination, or physical threats immediately halts the session and routes to corporate HR.
