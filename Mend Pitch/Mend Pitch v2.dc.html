02 · Why "the right way" fails

They tried to fix it the right way. They got labeled the problem.

An employee hits a very normal conflict with a teammate and tries to do the right thing: they set up one-on-ones with their managers to get support resolving it. Instead of being seen as thoughtful, what management takes away is that they were involved in a conflict. Conflict is bad for team harmony, so, the reasoning goes, maybe they are too.

Human processes are well meant, but they aren't built to stay contained. Talking to an AI about a personal conflict will feel awkward to some people. We say so on the first screen.

Awkward and genuinely private beats effective and quietly exposed.

Nothing recorded. Not recorded and then deleted.

You started it
