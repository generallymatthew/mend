A

Aurora Blob

teal pool, origin at top-left perimeter

Closest match to the reference clip: a soft teal/mint pool with its origin pulled out to the dome's own top-left edge, rather than floating inside the surface. Reads like light arriving from off-frame.

B

Diagonal Duotone

teal-to-pink, corner to corner

A full diagonal blend, teal in one corner and pink in the other with a warm band between. Teal band widened so it actually registers as a color, not a sliver that gets lost at the edge.

C

Mint Sheen

tighter mint highlight + rim shadow for depth

v2: a brighter, tighter mint specular dot instead of a soft wash, plus a darkened pool on the opposite rim. Reads as a curved, lit surface now instead of a flat pink fill with a faint tint.

D

Iridescent Sweep

conic teal/mint/pink, oil-slick

A conic sweep so the hue shifts as you go around the rim -- teal, mint, pink, back to teal. The most "alive" looking option, but also the busiest; worth checking in motion before committing.

E

Teal Top Cap

distinct teal zone, not blended

A separate teal pool capping the top of the dome like a little hat, rather than blending into the pink. Two-zone instead of a gradient -- simpler to reason about, might read as flatter in motion.

F

Dusk

teal rim darkening, no white highlight

No bright highlight at all -- the dome goes from a warm pink center to a muted teal/slate at the rim, like dusk light. Quietest of the six, closest in spirit to the brand's current flat language.
