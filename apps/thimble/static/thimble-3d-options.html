⌂ Launchpad
A

Soft Sheen

one faint highlight

The lightest touch: a single soft highlight, nothing darkened. Barely reads as "lit" at a glance, mostly just softens the flat fill slightly. Lowest risk of looking like a material change.

B

Glossy Dome

specular highlight + pooled shadow

A tight bright highlight plus a darker pool toward the rim -- reads like glazed ceramic or glass. The most literal "a real thimble, lit" of the four, and the biggest visual departure from the current flat brand mark.

C

Matte Shade

ambient rim darkening, no specular dot

No bright highlight at all, just a soft overall darkening toward the edges. Reads like soft rubber or fabric rather than glass or metal -- closer in spirit to the brand's current flat, friendly language than B or D.

D

Rim Light

edge-wrapping light + center highlight

Light wrapping the upper-left rim instead of one highlight pool -- the most "premium app icon" looking option. Watch this one in particular: the busiest background of the four, most likely to fight the mouth/ripples in motion.
