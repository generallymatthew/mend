Before you start

Mend helps you work through conflict at work. Talk it through privately with Thimble, no HR, no manager, nothing saved. A few ways people use it:

  • Discuss a conflict you had with a coworker, teammate, or manager.
  • Role-play a difficult conversation before having it in person.
  • Get help managing direct reports.
Not legal or HR advice. Thimble is an AI, not a professional. If something is urgent or unsafe, contact your actual HR department, a lawyer, or emergency services.
