Walk into the hard conversation already knowing what's going on.

A direct report needs tough feedback. Two people on your team can't work together. Or you're trying to understand how a conflict started. There's no neutral person to think it through with, and getting it wrong costs trust. Thimble is that second opinion, privately, before you're in the room.

Who this is for

Different situations. The same need: someone neutral to check it with first.

A hard performance conversation
You know you have to say something. You don't know how to say it without it going sideways.
Two people who can't work together
You weren't in the room for whatever happened, and you don't want to play judge.
A message you're not sure about
Before you hit send, you want to know how it actually reads, not just how you meant it.

Why it matters

Getting it wrong costs more than the conversation.

Nobody trained you to handle conflict between the people you manage. There's no feedback loop for how you're doing it — so the instinct is to avoid it, improvise it, or escalate it too early. Any of those costs the team more than the five minutes it would've taken to get it right.

For you
Confidence without formal training, and full autonomy over what you do with what you work out.
For the team
Unresolved tension doesn't stay contained — it leaks into the room and drags on everyone's focus.
