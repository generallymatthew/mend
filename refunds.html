Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective November 27, 2026 · Thimble LLC

The short version

  • Cancel any time. There are no contracts or cancellation fees.
  • Access continues until the end of the month you've already paid for.
  • Your first month comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee.

How to cancel

Email support@withthimble.com from your billing contact's address, or reply to any Stripe receipt, and say you'd like to cancel. We will confirm by email within one business day. Your subscription stops renewing, and your team keeps access until the end of the billing month you've already paid for.

Refunds

  • First month: if Thimble isn't right for your team, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund that payment in full.
  • After the first month: monthly payments are not refunded for partial months. Cancelling stops future charges.
  • Charged by mistake: if you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or charged the wrong amount, email us within 60 days and we will refund the incorrect amount.
  • If we stop offering Thimble: we will refund the unused part of your current month.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Changing your number of employees

To change the number of employees on a company-wide plan, email us. The new amount applies from your next billing date.
