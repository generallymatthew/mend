What you're about to see

Mend is a private, AI-facilitated way to work through coworker friction before it turns into an HR conversation. You'll land on a pitch page, then talk to Thimble, the AI facilitator, either by typing or by voice.

Nothing you say to Thimble is recorded or saved. If what you describe reads as real harassment, discrimination, or a threat, the session will route to a safety flow instead of continuing normally. That's intentional, not a bug.

How to go through it

  1. Read the pitch page first. Don't skip ahead to the session. Whatever you think of it, confused, skeptical, sold, that reaction is useful to me.
  2. Start a session. Either describe a real (small, low-stakes) piece of coworker friction you've actually experienced, or pick a scenario and roleplay it. Real friction, even minor, tends to produce more honest reactions than an invented one.
    Example scenarios
    • A peer repeatedly takes credit for shared work in front of the team.
    • A manager micromanages while telling you to "use your judgment."
    • A senior colleague sends off-hours messages that drift into uncomfortable territory.
  3. Talk to Thimble like you would a real tool. Interrupt it, correct it, go off-script, try voice if you can. I'd rather you break it than be polite to it.
  4. Finish the short survey at the end. It's quick, and it's the one piece of feedback that's structured enough for me to compare across testers.
