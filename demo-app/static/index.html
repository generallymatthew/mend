Your Mend flow

Each step opens its own local prototype in a new tab. Facilitator replies use your real Anthropic key, so keep each app's own server running per its README.

  1. 1
    Private processing
    Talk through what happened, on your own. The facilitator decides with you whether a joint session makes sense.
    /intake/
  2. 2
    Invite the other person (optional)
    A one-time link, not an email to their work address. You send it yourself, any way you like — Mend never delivers it and never sees who you sent it to.
    copied
  3. 3
    Suggest a conversation between two coworkers
    Not involved yourself? If you've noticed tension between two other people, generate two links and send one to each of them, any way you like — same as inviting someone yourself, just on their behalf. They'll each see it came from you.
    copied
  4. 4
    Joint session
    Whoever invited whom, this is where you both show up — each of you opens the session's own link with your own role, from separate devices or tabs.
    /joint/
  5. 5
    Post-session follow-up
    A private 1:1 afterward — handles any escalation disclosure first, then checks whether things actually got resolved.
    /post/
Sign-up checks a real access code and invites (direct or suggested) are real server-side records (crypto-shredded on accept/decline, per product-spec.html Section 3) — so an invite link works for a real second person on their own device, not just this same browser. Not built here: invite expiry or multi-org codes. The intake and joint-session apps each show a banner with a "continue" link once the facilitator's own signal says the next step is ready, so you don't have to guess when to move on.
