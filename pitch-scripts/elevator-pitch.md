# Elevator Pitch (30 Seconds)

**Tagline:** Heal the rift. Leave no trace.

## The pitch

> Mend is an AI tool that helps coworkers resolve everyday friction before it turns into an HR case. People vent privately first, then — if both sides agree — talk it through with Mend guiding the conversation so nobody dominates it. Nothing gets stored. It's like a dissolving suture: it holds things together while they heal, then disappears. If something crosses a real line — harassment, discrimination, a threat — it stops the session and sends it straight to HR instead of trying to mediate it.

## One-line versions by audience

- **To an investor:** "A confidential AI mediator for workplace friction that resolves the small stuff peer-to-peer and automatically escalates the real stuff to HR."
- **To an HR leader:** "A self-serve buffer that deflects routine peer friction from your inbox, with a hardcoded safety floor that guarantees real compliance issues still reach you."
- **To a friend:** "An app that helps two coworkers talk through a disagreement without either of them having to go to HR over something small."

## If they ask "isn't that just therapy for coworkers?"

> Not quite — there's no diagnosis, no ongoing relationship with it. It's closer to a structured negotiation session than therapy: it exists to get two people to a working agreement, then it's done.
