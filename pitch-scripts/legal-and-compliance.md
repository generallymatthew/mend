# Pitching Employment Counsel & Compliance Reviewers

Full outreach email and 1-page scoping memo already written: [../docs/pitch-strategy/10_LEGAL_COUNSEL_OUTREACH.md](../docs/pitch-strategy/10_LEGAL_COUNSEL_OUTREACH.md). Use that document directly for the actual outreach. This file is the short conversational version for when someone's already in the room (an incubator legal clinic session, a mentor who happens to be an employment attorney, etc).

## Opening line

> We've built an architecture-level answer to the liability question most conflict-resolution tools never address — zero persistent transcripts, and a hardcoded safety floor that halts the session the moment anything touches Title VII, harassment, or a physical threat. I'd like your read on whether that boundary is airtight before we take this to a real pilot.

## The three questions worth asking a reviewer directly

1. Does volatile, in-memory-only execution (no durable transcript storage) meaningfully reduce e-discovery/subpoena exposure for an employer deploying this, compared to a tool that logs everything?
2. Is the current safety-floor trigger taxonomy (Title VII discrimination, sexual harassment, physical threats, retaliation) complete, or are there categories a reviewer would add before this touches a real workplace?
3. What does the Terms of Service need to say about the boundary between "informal peer coaching" and anything that could later be characterized as the company's own investigation process?

## Framing the ask

> We're pre-pilot, not asking for a full engagement — a fixed-scope review of the scoping memo, or even a 20-minute gut check on whether we're missing an obvious risk, would be enough to move forward responsibly.
